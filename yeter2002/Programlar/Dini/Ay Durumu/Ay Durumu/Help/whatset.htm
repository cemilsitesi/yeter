MoonPhase Setup
Basically there are two very simple settings for MoonPhase:
I am in the southern hemisphere: As the moon is seen upside down in the southern hemisphere, you need to select this option to make the icon look like the way the moon looks in the sky.

Use photorealistic icons: MoonPhase has two sets of icons to display the moon phase. Either as a plain yellow circle, or as a representation of the moon's surface in textured gray-scale. Select this option to see black and white icons with shadows similar to the moon's surface.
