Using MoonPhase
Using MoonPhase is as painless as it gets with any software. Once you setup the program, you can almost forget about it and enjoy the icon views of the current lunar phases that appear on your taskbar.

If you move your mouse over the icon and hover for a moment, the ToolTip will show you the number of days the Moon is in its phases.

If you want more information at this time, left-click on the icon to display the Moon Phases information dialog. This dialog will give you information about the current lunar cycle. It will show the milestone phases and when they did, or will happen.

Click anywhere on this dialog, or hit the Escape key to make the dialog go away.
