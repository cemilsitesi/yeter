EditPad is available in several languages.
If you would rather have a version in another language,
feel free to download it from http://www.editpadclassic.com/

Languages currently available: (August 6th, 2000)
  * Dansk       (Danish)
  * Deutsch     (German)
  * Français    (French)
  * English     (English)
  * Español     (Spanish)
  * Italiano    (Italian)
  * Nederlands  (Dutch)
  * Norsk       (Norwegian)
  * Polski      (Polish)
  * Português   (Portuguese)
  * Suomi       (Finnish)
  * Svenska     (Swedish)
