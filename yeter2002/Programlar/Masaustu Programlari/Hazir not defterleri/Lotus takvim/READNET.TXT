For information on planning, installing and setting up Lotus Organizer
Release 2.1 on your network, see the Lotus Organizer Release 2.1
Administrator's Guide. The Lotus Organizer Release 2.1 Administrator's
Guide is intended for administrators who use Organizer mail-based or
single-server scheduling. It is not intended for Organizer PIM
(stand-alone) or SmartSuite administrators.

NDEPLOY.WRI and CDEPLOY.WRI are Windows Write files that answer common
deployment questions for administrators who use Lotus Organizer
Release 2.1 for group scheduling with Lotus Notes or cc:Mail,
respectively. The files are not intended for administrators who use
Organizer 2.1 for single-server scheduling, shared calendaring with
no group scheduling, or Lotus SmartSuite administrators. By default,
the files are located in the \LOTUS\ORG2\ADMIN directory.

TECHNOTE.OR2 provides information for Organizer Release 2.1
administrators and advanced users. By default, the file is located in
the \LOTUS\ORG2\ORGFILES directory.
