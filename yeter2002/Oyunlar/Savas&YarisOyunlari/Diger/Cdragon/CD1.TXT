THE *PLOT*
==========
Cosmo Dragon decided not to join the Halloween party this year. He had gotten
fed up with it. His "friends" didn't take no for an answer. They did take
something else however. Cosmo Jr, the cute baby dragon, Cosmo's son. This,
Cosmo couldn't handle. Something snapped in his mind. The once friendly dragon
went crazy. He would stop at nothing to get back his son.

WHY OH WHY
==========
Why I wrote this game? >>Yes, I'd like to know why you bothered<< Well,
actually, I didn't. It just happened, >>That sounds scary<< it was almost
finished when I realised what I was doing. >>Making game players' lives
generally miserable?<< Indeed! Anyway, I had drawn the animation of a flying
dragon. I tested it, but felt something was missing. Therefore, I added the
starry background and the asteroids. Keyboard controls followed and I already
had a small game. As usual it got completely out of hand >>I'll say!<< and
this is the result.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
===================
- 386 or better.
- 2MB of RAM.
- SoundBlaster version 1.5 or better required for music and sound effects.

INSTRUCTIONS
============
Use the cursor keys to change Cosmo's direction. Press the spacebar to make
him shoot a fireball. When Cosmo's run out of lives, the game over message
appears. Press enter to start all over again. To quit the game, press escape.

BONUSES
=======
There are several bonuses Cosmo can pick up. They look like shiny orbs with
a letter on them. The letters mean the followin:
  A - Auto fire
  F - Fly faster
  L - Extra life
  M - 1000 bonus points
  P - Protective shield
  W - Weapon upgrade
  ? - Random selection
