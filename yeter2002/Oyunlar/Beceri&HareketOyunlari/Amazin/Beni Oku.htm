AMAZIN' SPISPOPD v2.0

---------------------

 

HOW TO PLAY

 

         The object of the game is to collect

all of the Candles on each level.  This task

is complicated by a group of angry Pumpkins.

If a Pumpkin touches you, you lose one life.

Luckily, on each level there are Hammers you

can collect.  Each Hammer lasts a certain length

of time before disappearing, and while you have

it, you smash every Pumpkin you come into contact

with. Each level is more difficult than the

previous, with faster Pumpkins and Hammers that

don't last as long.

         You can also play with a friend.  This

gives you a better chance to collect all the

Candles, but you have to be careful - you both

share one set of lives, so feel free to yell at

your partner every time they get killed.

 

---------------------------------------------

GAME MODES

 

         There are four different modes of play in

Amazin' SPISPOPD.  They are available from the

main menu at the title screen.

 

Happy Fields

         There are 20 levels of Happy Fields.  If

         you collect all the Candles in all of them,

         you win.  You can play with one player or

         two.

Dank Dungeons

         The Dank Dungeons go on forever, getting

         bigger and tougher each time.  The objective

         is simply to get as far as you can.  One

         or two players may play this mode.

Sibling Rivalry

         In this mode, you and another player go

         head to head, in a race to collect the

         most Candles.  There are a number of

         different options which can be set, such as

         whether or not Pumpkins are on the levels

         as well, how often Power-Ups appear, and

         what type of levels you wish to play on. 

         You can play on randomly generated levels

         like the Dank Dungeons, or on the Happy

         Fields levels.  Each time you get hit, you

         lose 1 candle.  If you smash your opponent,

         you gain one candle!  There's no limit on

         lives, the level simply continues until all

         the candles are collected, and whoever has

         the most wins.  You can play single matches,

         or multiple rounds.  You can only play this

         mode with two players.

Time Attack

         Select any of the Happy Fields levels, or

         one of the first 20 Dank Dungeons levels. 

         A clock in the corner of the screen lets you

         know how long you're taking.  The object is

         simply to finish the level in record time. 

         Record times are kept for each level

         separately.  You have unlimited lives in this

         mode, but each time you are hit, you get a

         10-second penalty added to your time.  This

         mode can only be played with one player.

 

---------------------------------------------

CONTROLS

 

         The default controls are the arrow keys for

Player 1, and the keys W, A, D, and X for Player 2. 

You can change these keys in the Options menu, or

select a joystick instead.  The main menu can be

perused with either Player's controls, or with the

arrow keys, or the mouse.  Press Enter or a Joystick

button to select menu items.

 

---------------------------------------------

POWER-UPS

 

         These items appear on the playing field

occasionally, and are well worth picking up!

 

Points (100, 250, 500, 1000)

         These just increase your score.

Speedy Boots

         These temporarily grant you super speed.

Ice Cream

         This triggers a blizzard that slows down

         the Pumpkins.

Zzz

         Puts the pumpkins to sleep temporarily.

Ghost

         Gives you the ability to walk through

         walls for a short time.

Hammer Up

         The same as picking up a normal Hammer.

Shield

         Temporarily makes you invincible.

1UP

         A free life!

 

The following Power-Ups only appear in Sibling

Rivalry mode.

 

Big Candle

         Counts as five candles.

Evil Eye

         Causes a heart attack in anyone that you

         look at.  Great fun.

Fire Power

         Everywhere you walk, you leave a trail of

         deadly flames.

Ice Power

         Everywhere you walk, you leave ice cubes,

         which act as walls until they melt.

NOTES

 

* Be careful around teleporters.  You can be

  crushed by anyone coming through one, even

  if you have a Hammer.  You can also use this

  feature to smash Pumpkins without a Hammer,

  if your timing is perfect.

 

* In Time Attack mode, the 10 second penalty

  for being hit really adds up.  It's worth

  going out of your way to stay alive.

 

* You can use Time Attack mode to practice at

  higher levels without having to get through

  the lower levels first.

 

* Yes, there are cheat codes.

 

* Each pumpkin has its own technique for

  chasing you.  Learning these can help you

  avoid them.
