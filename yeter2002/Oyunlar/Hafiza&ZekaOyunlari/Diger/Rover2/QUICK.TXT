^C^I*                      Notice to SVGA users!                       *

^C^1****************************************
^C^1TO ALL USERS WITH SVGA GRAPHICS ADAPTERS
^C^1****************************************

If your computer has an SVGA graphics card, you may see some unwanted graphics effects when you begin to play the actual game.  
^CThere is nothing to worry about if that happens. 

To correct any problems with the graphics display of this game, type the following to begin play:

^C^1START /COMP

This should clear up any graphics display problems you might see on your SVGA system!


^C^I*                       System requirements                           *

Computer type: 
   An XT, 286, 386, or 486 processor.

Memory: 
   You need 640K of conventional memory with at least 508K free.

Graphics mode: 
   EGA graphics required (supported on EGA, VGA, & SVGA cards).

Sound support: 
   PC internal speaker sound; AdLib; Sound Blaster; SoundSource.

Game controls: 
   The game is controlled by use of your keyboard or joystick.
